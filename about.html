About

Milana — PMU artist in the UAE

Russian permanent makeup and camouflage artist, working at clients' homes across Dubai and Sharjah.

I work with three things: brows, lips and camouflage. That is deliberate. Camouflage in particular is slow, technical work — matching a pigment to skin, watching how it heals, correcting on the next session — and it does not sit comfortably alongside a menu of twenty treatments.

Every appointment starts with your skin rather than with a pigment chart. Undertone is read in daylight, the mix is made on the day, and it is tested on you before the first pass. On camouflage it is tested and left to heal before a full area is started.

And I come to you. Home service is not a compromise here: it means you heal in your own space, without a drive across the city in the sun on day one.

How I work

Three rules I don't break

Shape before pigment

Nothing is tattooed until you have seen the drawn shape in a mirror and agreed to it. If we cannot agree on the shape, we do not start — and you pay nothing.

Lighter than the target

Pigment darkens as it heals, so the first session builds a base deliberately under the final colour. The touch-up is where the colour is set. Anyone who finishes in one pass is guessing.

Honest about what is possible

Some scars should not be tattooed, and some brows need a correction before anything new goes in. You get that answer before you pay a deposit, not after.

Safety

Sterile, single-use, opened in front of you

A home service does not mean relaxed standards. The setup that arrives at your door is the setup you would expect to see in a studio.

  • Needles and cartridges are single-use and unsealed in front of you
  • Pigment cups, gloves, razors and liners are disposable
  • Barrier film on every surface and on the machine
  • Medical history and contraindications checked before the date is confirmed
  • A patch test before any camouflage area is started
  • Written aftercare, and a check-in during the healing week
