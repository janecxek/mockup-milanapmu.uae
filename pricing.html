In the price

What every appointment includes

Sterile setup

Needles, cartridges, pigment cups and gloves are single-use and unsealed in front of you.

A pigment mixed for you

Colour is mixed on the day against your skin, not chosen from a chart in advance.

The visit itself

Travel to your address in Dubai or Sharjah is included in the price.

Aftercare and follow-up

Written aftercare, a check-in during healing, and my number for anything that worries you.
